    The comparison

    Popularity: count training ratings ≥4. Item CF: shrinkage-adjusted cosine similarities between positive interaction columns. PyTorch BPR: user and item embeddings, item bias, pairwise softplus loss, and sampled-parameter L2 regularization.

    Time comes first

    Train before 1 Feb 1998 UTC. Validate from 1 Feb to 15 Mar. Test from 15 Mar onward. The model stays frozen; all ratings before the evaluation cutoff are excluded from recommendations. No random interaction split.

    What counts as relevant?

    Ratings ≥4 are positives. Every train-observed movie is eligible unless previously rated. Negatives are uniformly sampled training-catalog items with no training rating from that user. Unknown does not mean disliked. Future ratings never shape negative sampling.

    Cold start, honestly

    Unseen users receive popularity in the benchmark. Unseen items are excluded and counted. The demo averages normalized favorite item vectors. This is a separate heuristic, not a learned user embedding; its proxy results are reported separately.

    Model card · intended use, evidence, and limitations

    Intended use: independent research and recruiter review. No commercial deployment, safety-critical decisions, or personalized profiling of real visitors.

    Training: seed 42, CPU PyTorch, Adam, BPR softplus loss. Dimensions, epoch and CF shrinkage selected using validation only. See the saved configuration and selection log in the source repository.

    Limits: historical, self-selected ratings; no exposures, propensity weights, impression logs, or randomized assignment. Cold-user prevalence and exclusion of new movies limit generalization. One split and seed do not establish robustness.

    Privacy & licensing: no user embeddings, ratings, titles from MovieLens, or learned MovieLens item vectors in the published site. Local bundles are user-supplied. The fictional sandbox is a separate, self-authored demonstration.

    Cold-start proxy: the results JSON includes favorite_fold_in, evaluated with all positive training history as favorites. This does not validate a visitor selecting only 2–5 favorites; that remains unmeasured.

    Monitoring: evaluate data drift, catalog freshness, exposure concentration, empty outputs, latency, and feedback loops before any real rollout.

    04 / THE NEXT PRODUCT DECISION

    Offline promise.
    Online proof still needed.

    A randomized discovery experiment

    With suitable commercial data rights, randomize eligible signed-in users 50/50 between popularity and the validation-selected candidate. Keep assignment stable and the recommendation surface identical.

    Primary: qualified plays per randomized eligible user over 14 days; define a qualified play as ≥10 minutes. Log impressions and use intention-to-treat analysis.

    Guardrails: p95 serving latency, errors, dismissals, repeat recommendations, creator exposure concentration, and catalog coverage. Pre-register sample size, minimum detectable effect, stopping rules, and decision thresholds.

    No engagement lift, revenue impact, or online latency improvement is claimed by this project.
